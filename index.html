A card takes a flat cut that hurts most on small tickets; a platform holds your money between charges. This is the alternative: one approval becomes N scheduled payments — non-custodial, no invoice, no per-period signature. Live on Base.

The whole flow, in about 20 seconds

  1. Pick a ticket and a period. The page reads the live fee and shows you the exact math — what your counterparty receives and what the toll is.~10s
  2. Sign ONE approve(). That single transaction is the whole mandate. There is no per-period signature and no new contract to deploy.~10s
  3. A keeper fires the laps. Each period it calls the already-deployed factory; each lap is its own small vault holding one ticket. A missed period is a hole, not a debt.

Why you can check it, not just trust it

1
Nothing to connect to read it
Every link on this page works read-only. The fee, the owner, the code size — all fetched straight from Base. No wallet, no account, no sign-up.
2
We never hold the money
Each period is a separate on-chain vault. Funds sit in that vault, not in our account, and the counterparty withdraws what has vested.
3
The fee can't be quietly raised past its cap
It is a storage variable an owner can change — but the contract hard-caps it at — bps. You always read the current value live, so the page can't show you a stale one.
4
A real receipt exists, on-chain
A settled payment can mint exactly one receipt NFT to the payer — the proof of payment lives on the chain, not on a server. One is already minted: read the mint tx ↗
5
You can decide before you sign
Build the mandate first — the page shows the exact approve() calldata and a QR to hand it to the payer. Nothing is on chain until someone chooses to send it.

What this does not claim

It is early. The contracts are live and were exercised on Base mainnet by our own keys — which puts an honest asterisk on the numbers:
  • Every run so far is fleet-internal: the payer and the counterparty are both wallets we control. Money moved and the fee is real, but a stranger's wallet has not paid it yet.
  • There is no "trusted by" logo row, because there is nothing to prove yet. The claims above are all checkable individually instead.
  • Small tickets have a floor: below roughly $0.31 a period's fee is smaller than its own gas. The subscription page shows this line and tells you when your ticket is under it.
