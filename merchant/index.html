Two different problems — pick yours

1 · If the risk is the other person

Pay a stranger, with a way out.

Someone has to go first: send and hope, or trust-me-first. A money-app dispute is a coin-flip run by a company that was not there. Instead: lock the money against a condition — a secret, or an arbiter you both name. The funds sit in a contract, not with us.

What you get: released only when the condition is met; if it never is, after the deadline anyone can push the refund — including you. No one can keep it; no one has to be asked. The fee is taken on release only — a deal that falls through costs nothing. An agent can pay this too, with no signup.

2 · If the tax is the card's fixed 30¢

Charge $2 a month without a card eating 36¢ of it.

A card processor takes 2.9% + 30¢ on every charge — and that 30¢ does not care how small the charge is. On a $2 ticket that is 36¢ gone, 18% of the money, before anything else. Same small amount, again and again: a coach, a Discord, a newsletter.

What you keep: on that same $2 sub we take 10¢ where the card takes 36¢ — you keep 26¢ more, every month, forever. One approval, then it runs: no monthly nag, no processor to be approved by.

What it costs — and who actually pays it

One rate, — bps = 5%, taken from the amount that moves. Nobody is surcharged on top. The payer sends the sticker price; the recipient nets 95%; a separate address receives the 5%.

The payer pays the ticket, exactly as agreed — $2.00, nothing added.
The recipient nets the amount minus the fee — $1.90 on a $2 ticket.
A separate address receives the fee $0.10 → reading…

On escrow the fee is on a release only — a refund returns 100%. The rate is owner-settable and read live above, and the contract caps it. No monthly platform fee.

Who this is not for (the honest part)

Read this before you switch. This is not strictly better than a card — it is better in specific places, and worse in others.
  • Big one-off invoices. Above about $14 the card's percentage beats our flat 5%: on a $100 invoice a card is ~$3.20 and we are $5. If your whole business is a few large invoices, a card processor is genuinely fine.
  • Anyone already on a ~1% crypto processor. On price alone we lose to them at any size — we are 5% to their 1%. We are not selling the cheapest number; we are selling the bundle: escrow, one link, no signup.
  • People who never touch a wallet and never want to. Some steps live in a wallet. If interacting with one makes you angry, that is friction you did not ask for.
  • Anything needing a bank statement, a chargeback, or credit. We are not a bank and not a card. No chargeback here — the escrow is the substitute.
