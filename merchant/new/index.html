Paste where you want to be paid. Get a link + QR your customer opens — they pay in one tap. No wallet, no account, nothing to deploy. Mainnet (Base) by default.

The QR encodes the same pay link. A stranger scans or taps it and pays your address; the 5% is subtracted on the way in and goes to a separate address — you get the rest, they install nothing.

How it works (60 seconds)

  1. You paste your address → you get …/payme/?to=<your addr>.
  2. You send that link (or QR) to whoever owes you money.
  3. They open it → the page derives a deposit address that forwards to you → they pay. The 5% fee is charged on the money you receive, on the way in.

The link carries your address, so it needs no server and no sign-up. Fee is read live from the chain by the pay page (feeBps() = 500 on Base); a dead RPC shows a labelled baked value, never a fake live one.
