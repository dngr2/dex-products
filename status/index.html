Known operational notes

  • Every retired factory on Base is paused — v1 and the pre-relaunch v2.1 line. They still hold existing positions; withdrawals are unaffected.
  • Ownership of the NEW line is a fresh, unexposed key; the pre-relaunch line is still owned by the exposed key (frozen, not moved). What the relaunch did not reach is itemised on Security.
  • Fees and the treasury are derived from the chain at build (fees.json); this page reads only code-presence, not fees.
